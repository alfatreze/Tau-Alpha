Chladni Plate Lab

Preview of three Chladni-pattern meters for Tau Alpha, drawn at the real 400x110 meter box in RGB565. The default layout computes one tile and replicates it across the box with plain copies. Includes a blit-engine cost model, an audio headroom check and a benchmark on a fixed demo track. Nothing here is on hardware yet.

Lower strip: 16 band levels (bass left), then spectral rise over the last 6 s. Amber ticks are triggers.

Pure modes, cos(mπx)cos(nπy) ± cos(nπx)cos(mπy), computed with the same Q14 kernel the meter uses. Label is m,n and family sign.

Per-frame cost (30 fps, running average)

Blit cycle figures are estimates from the engine's RTL structure, not measurements. The blit-storm Check (CT_BLT) is the gate that turns them into numbers. Constants: 100 MHz SDRAM, 60 MHz CPU, 6 cycles per multiply-add, 48 cycles per uncached window store, 24 SDRAM cycles per single-word engine transaction, 40 cycles per rect command.

Benchmark on the demo track

Steady change: share of plate cells that toggle per frame away from onsets. Punch: the same, in the 6 frames after a trigger, divided by steady. A good meter has low steady change and punch well above 1.
