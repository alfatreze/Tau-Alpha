## A012/2 · R-1 to R-5 are built (all host-proven, nothing on a Pocket: it is a lab feature)
- **R-1 embed mode:** `{type:"tau-lab-embed", on:true}` (or the URL fragment `#embed`): no heading, panel, parameter list, split strip or details area, transparent, no sticky stage, **the canvas cropped to the current view and drawn at its own pixel size** (no border); the page posts `tau-lab-size {view, rect, w, h}`. You can drop your stylesheet and the sizing part of the bridge. The split strip (Layered Wave's boundary editor) is hidden too, since it changes parameters you own.
- **R-2 options:** `tau-lab-options {meter, options:[{id,label,type,choices|min,max,value,applies_to}]}` after ready and after each set/option, and `{type:"tau-lab-option", id, value}` answered by `tau-lab-option-applied {id,value,clamped}` or `error` (`unknown option`, `not a number`, `not for this meter`). Nine options: theme, mode, accent, source (demo, sweep, silence: the ones that need no file or microphone), `fullfig`, `barready` (Winamp Bars), `timedg` (Layered Wave), `statsok` and `corr` (Sound Map); `applies_to` says which meters, so you need not hard-code it.
- **R-3 info as data:** `tau-lab-info {meter, cost:{mean,max,budget,class,over,verdict,basis,vs_bars,cell_width,cpu_low,cpu_high,engine_kpx}, table:[{key,label,options:[{value,selected,cpu_low,cpu_high,delta,kops}]}]}` after setup and each change (about 0.5 s later) and on `tau-lab-info-get`; the table no longer needs `#infod` to be open.
- **R-4 views:** `lab-data.json` `views`: `rects.small` `{16,152,368,122}`, `rects.full` `{0,0,400,323}`, and `meters[<id>].small|full`. The firmware's fullscreen view draws whichever meter is selected, so **every selectable meter has a full view** (your toggle for all is right; retired meters have none). `schema.meters` is untouched. Sizes and x are the firmware's (checked by a host test); the small view's y is the lab's own 152, the firmware's Now Playing meter starts at 154 (a two-pixel offset of the lab's canvas; the golden vectors are drawn in this box, so we did not move it).
- **R-5 cost message:** `tau-lab-cost {meter, frame, window:{n,mean,max}, recent:{n,mean,max}, budget, class, over}` every other frame (once you have sent any `tau-lab-*` message). **The verdict window is the worst of the last 120 frames** against the class budget (`over = window.max > budget`); `recent` is the last 30 frames for a fast bar. You need not hook `TauCost.summarize`.
