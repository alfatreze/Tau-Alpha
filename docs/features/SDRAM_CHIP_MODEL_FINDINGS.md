# SDRAM chip model: findings (ambiguities in the contract / sheet, and numbers the model produced)

Author: the chip-model agent, clean-room (contract v0.2 sections 1, 2, 3.3-3.5, 3.7, 7.1; `SDRAM_PART_FACTS.md`; the sibling-part sheet AS4C32M16MS-6BIN Rev 1.0, not the MSA variant). Model: `sim/sdram_chip_model.sv`; tests: `sim/sdram_chip_model/` (`run_tests.sh`). Everything below is **simulation only**; nothing was run on a Pocket.

Part A lists each point where the contract is ambiguous or contradicts the sheet: the two readings, what the model assumed, and a flag. Part B lists results the analytical checks produced that the contract marked "est.".

## Part A: ambiguities and contradictions

| # | Point | Reading 1 | Reading 2 | Model assumed | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | PREA with open banks (3.4 vs 3.7) | 3.4: "REF and PREA need every bank idle" | 3.7 preemption issues BST, PREA, tRP, REF to close banks; sheet: Precharge All is legal from "Any" state | PREA is legal with open banks (it closes them), subject to tRAS / tDPL / tWR of each open bank; REF needs idle banks and tRP after PREA | **contract text of 3.4 should say "REF needs every bank idle"** |

## Part B: results the checks produced (contract "est." items)

All at 100 MHz, FPGA output delay 1.5 .. 3.5 ns, OE tco 1.5 .. 3.5 ns, tOH 1.8 ns, board delay 0 unless stated.

1. **Valid window of word 0** (relative to the controller edge that launches the READ, the model's own measurement): CL2 `[2.30, 2.68]` tCK (tAC 8 ns); CL3 `[3.00, 3.68]` tCK (tAC 5 ns). Matches the contract's estimate (the contract rounds the end up with tOH 2.5 ns).
2. **Capture edge:** FALL (the falling edge of `clk`) is inside at CL2 and CL3 with margins **2.0 ns setup / 1.8 ns hold at CL2** (tAC 8 ns) and 5.0 / 1.8 ns at CL3. RISE has **no edge inside the CL2 window at worst-case tAC** (test `n_cap_rise_worst` fails as expected); with tAC 4 ns a rising edge captures (test `rw_data_rise_cl2_tac4`, margin 1.0 ns setup). So `FALL` as the default is confirmed analytically for the worst case.
3. **Board delay budget for FALL:** a spread of 0 .. 1.5 ns passes (`cap_delay_ok`), 0 .. 2.5 ns fails (`n_cap_delay_late`, the falling edge lands before the data is valid); a capture clock 2 ns late fails the hold side (`n_cap_skew_hold`). So the usable board delay plus capture skew budget at CL2 is about +2.0 ns setup side and about +1.8 ns hold side (the sum of the two is the 3.8 ns window width).
4. **Read to write turnaround (3.5).** With the earliest WR measured in controller cycles from the cycle that presents the RD, n words read, L = CL + n - 1: **CL2: conflict-free from L+2 (CL+n+1), a WR at L+1 conflicts** (the chip may drive until L+0.8 tCK, the FPGA enable arrives 1.5 ns after its edge); **CL3 (tHZ 5 ns): conflict-free from L+1 (CL+n)**, L conflicts. This matches contract 3.5 for CL2.
5. **Gap in 3.5 for CL3:** "DQM high from L-1 and the WR itself ends the read" does **not** work at CL3: the slot w+CL-1 = w+2 is still launched and DQM at the WR cycle must be low (write word 0), so the chip drives while the FPGA writes (test `n_turn_cl3_wrterm` raises `SE_BUS_CONFLICT`). At CL3 the read must be ended by a BST (or the write's first word masked) before the WR. 3.5 was written for CL2 only ("est. at 100 MHz, CL2"); the PHY author needs the CL3 rule.
6. **Read burst termination:** BST, RD (any column) or PRE of the burst bank at r+n ends a read after exactly n words at CL2 and CL3 (tests `rd_counts_cl2/cl3`). **DQM high at the BST/PRE cycle masks the last in-flight word at CL3** (DQM at edge e masks the slot e+2, the last wanted slot is c+CL-1): a PHY must keep DQM low in the terminating cycle at CL3, or start masking from L-1 where L is the last wanted slot, as 3.5 says.
7. **Write to read:** RD at w+n (tCDL 1) after n words passes with the FPGA OE dropping at tco_max 3.5 ns; the chip starts driving at 1.5 ns + tLZ after the second edge, no conflict (`wr_rd_turn_cl2/cl3`).
8. **Refresh:** a full 64 ms run with one REF per 780 clk (8,450 refreshes) raises no `SE_TREF` (`legal_tref_full`, about 2.5 minutes); 65 ms without REF raises it (`n_tref_full`). The limit is 64 ms + 8 x 7.8 us.
9. **Simulators:** iverilog 13 (`-g2012`) runs everything. Verilator 5.052 lints the model without errors, but it is 2-state and the test bench's X-valued pin envelopes (the setup/hold and drive windows) need a 4-state simulator, so the tests are not run with it. Build paths with spaces break Verilator's make; build elsewhere if it is ever wanted.
10. **Messages:** 12 individually-named rules of 3.4 plus init, CKE, setup/hold, tCK, tREF, tRAS(max), conflict and capture each have a negative test (42 negative tests and 23 positive tests in `run_tests.sh`; 2 more long runs).
