## Gotchas learned
- Never reuse a meter key: `meters/waterfall` is the old player's retired meter (index 1); the new one is `waterfall_grid` (index 17, `p2_only`).
- Meter order = saved index = pack slot: append only. Per-meter persisted preset fields (`SF_MPRE0..5`) must exist for every meter (`_Static_assert` in `appearance.c`).
- `node tools/meters/preview/test.js` hangs under `make` without stdin closed (Makefile now `</dev/null`).
- A Quartus fit was not needed for any of this (no RTL change); bitstream = `fontram-b810`.
